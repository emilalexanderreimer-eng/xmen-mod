# X-Men Mutants (Minecraft mod)

A NeoForge mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1** where every player is a mutant.

## Install it with CurseForge

1. Download the newest `xmen-*.jar` from the [Latest build](../../releases/tag/latest) release.
2. In the CurseForge app, go to **Minecraft → Create Custom Profile**, pick **Minecraft 1.21.1** and the **NeoForge** loader, and create it.
3. On the profile, click the **⋮** menu → **Open Folder**, then open the `mods` folder (create it if it isn't there).
4. Drop the `.jar` into `mods` and press **Play**.

## Playing

The first time you join a world you get a random power from a pool of 300, shown in the top-left corner.

- `/power` shows your power and what it does.
- `/power list` shows how many powers each level has; `/power list omega` lists the Omega ones.
- `/power set <power>` and `/power random` change your power, but only in creative mode.
- Instant Travel mutants can also type `/power travel <x> <y> <z>`.
- Press **R** to use your power (you can change the key in Options → Controls). Your cooldown shows under the power name.

### All 300 powers

Every power works. "R" means you press your power key.

## Structures

16 X-Men places spawn in new worlds (or in new, unexplored chunks of old worlds). Find one fast with
`/locate structure xmen:x_mansion` (swap in any name below).

| Structure | Where | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| `x_mansion` | plains, meadows, forests (rare) | Movie-style castle with a round tower, fountain and basketball court. The X-Men live there with their own skins (Professor X in his wheelchair sells suits and the Mutant Serum). The elevator in the dining hall goes down to the silver level: X-doors that open by themselves, Cerebro (a giant sphere that lights up and shows mutants as red dots), the Danger Room (three waves of real training robots, flying laser drones and a Sentinel), med lab, war room, suit room and the hangar with a flyable X-Jet. The court floor slides open with the Hangar Control. Krakoa gate in the corner garden. |
| `apocalypse_pyramid` | desert (very rare) | A huge pyramid like the one in *X-Men: Apocalypse*: gold capstone with a crystal tip, an avenue of obelisks, two giant statues at the gate. Inside, the Four Horsemen (Storm, Psylocke, Archangel, Magneto) guard the corners and Apocalypse sleeps on his altar until you come close; then sunlight shoots down through the capstone and the gold veins light up. He has a health bar with numbers, sinks you into sand, blasts, teleports, calls Clan Akkaba and grows at half health. ## Building it yourself

GitHub builds the jar automatically on every push. To build locally you need Java 21: run `./gradlew build` and the jar lands in `build/libs/`.
